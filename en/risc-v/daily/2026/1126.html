Today’s Focus: ESWIN integrated circuit industrial base lands in Beijing Yizhuang (11-25)

On November 25, the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area Administrative Committee and Beijing ESWIN Technology Group signed an economic development cooperation agreement, bringing the ESWIN Group integrated circuit industrial base project to Beijing Yizhuang.

The project is centered on chip design and coordinates with computing infrastructure construction, silicon-based and new materials R&D, and semiconductor equipment, among other segments. The stated plan is to vertically connect “chip—board—server—computing cluster,” covering three scenarios: cloud-edge intelligence, embodied intelligence, and endpoint intelligence.

The two signing parties position this as a RISC-V full-stack computing industry cluster. ESWIN Group Chairman Wang Dongsheng said the company will increase R&D investment and advance the large-scale deployment of RISC-V computing architecture chips and computing systems.

ESWIN Computing takes RISC-V + AI as its foundation, with products in three lines: endpoint intelligence, embodied intelligence, and cloud-edge intelligence. At ICCAD-Expo 2026 on November 19, the company disclosed that it has accumulated 620+ IPs and 20+ RISC-V core families.

Beijing E-Town currently hosts more than 400 integrated circuit-related enterprises, covering design, manufacturing, packaging and testing, equipment, materials, and components. In the first three quarters of this year, the district’s integrated circuit output value exceeded RMB 160 billion.

Date: 2026-11-25 Source: https://www.ncsti.gov.cn/kjdt/scyq/bjjjjskfq/jkdt/202611/t20261125_265445.html. See also: https://www.eswin.com/news/newsinfo/179.html.

RISC-V 100 Leaderboard Updates

SpacemiT V100 enters the RISC-V SPEC CPU2006 board for the first time (11-24)

On November 24, riscv100.top’s What’s New posted an entry: SpacemiT V100 XSCC 1.1 SPEC CPU2006 Speed.

The absolute scores given in the announcement are int 17.879 and fp 15.297. The mode is Speed/Base, the input is ref, and all 29 integer and floating-point tests were completed. The leaderboard entry is dated 2026-10-10, earlier than this inspection day, making it a backfilled entry rather than a new run from the day.

The leaderboard displays Ratio/GHz, with corresponding entries of int 8.127 and fp 6.953.

The V100 is SpacemiT’s RISC-V server processor, supporting RVA23. The official product page lists 46 cores per chip: 6 XiangShan Kunminghu V2R2 cores and 40 SpacemiT X100 cores.

Testing ran on a dual-socket V100 node with the openRuyi Creek operating system, using only the X100 cores.

Against the current leaderboard, the top spot in the RISC-V channel’s SPEC CPU2006 remains XiangShan KMHv3 with XSCC 2.0. That entry is dated 2026-10-17, with int 22.802 and fp 25.649, also Ratio/GHz.

Date: 2026-11-24 Source: https://riscv100.top/whats-new/2026-11-24-v100-xscc11-spec06-speed. See also: https://riscv100.top/riscv/spec06/.

Date: 2026-11-25 Source: https://github.com/openRuyi-Project/kernel-team-tools/commits/master.

RevyOS kernel: 20 config commits merged in bulk, covering SoCs from six vendors (11-25)

RevyOS’s linux repo (revyos/7.3.y) merged 20 commits that day, with commit times clustered around 19:41.

New SoC configs include Zhihe A210, ESWIN EIC7700, UltraRISC DP1000, SpacemiT K1 and K3, DAMO XuanTie TH1520, and Sophgo SG2042 and SG2044.

The same day enabled ARM SCMI, switched the EFI framebuffer to efidrm, and enabled drm_panic. It also added a linuxboot config and debian_defconfig.

There was also a K3 errata commit: replacing WRS.NTO with WRS.STO. Another commit enabled fan control for the ESWIN starpro64.

Author dates on this batch range from 2025-12 to 2026-11-20, making it a one-time merge of a long-running config branch rather than work written that day.

Date: 2026-11-25 Source: https://github.com/revyos/linux/commits/revyos/7.3.y.

ESWIN: ICCAD showcases 620+ IP items and 20+ RISC-V core series (11-19)

Background. At ICCAD-Expo 2026 on November 19–20, ESWIN Computing chairman Mi Peng gave a keynote titled “RISC-V+AI Reconstructing Digital Infrastructure.”

The company says it has accumulated 620+ IP items and 20+ RISC-V core series, covering six capability categories: intelligent perception, intelligent interaction, smart connectivity, multimedia processing, cognitive decision-making, and intelligent control. Its technology foundation is called RISACon, with software split into three layers: RISEA, RISAA, and RISCA. Its three growth curves correspond to endpoint, embodied, and cloud-edge.

V. Summary and Trend Observations

  • Industry signing: The only actual signing today was in Yizhuang. ESWIN is establishing its base in the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area, focused on RISC-V chip design and full-stack compute clusters. The chain cited by the signatories runs from chips and boards to servers and compute clusters.
  • New platform on the leaderboard: SpacemiT’s V100 enters RISC-V SPEC CPU2006 for the first time. The machine’s composition is worth noting: of each 46 cores, 6 are XiangShan Kunminghu V2R2 and 40 are SpacemiT X100. This test used only the X100 cores, and the leaderboard score is Ratio/GHz 8.127 (int).
  • Distro picks up server validation: This test ran on openRuyi Creek. On the same day, the main openRuyi repo merged 20 packaging changes, and kernel-team-tools advanced kernel tags. The software stack and hardware validation are starting to line up.
  • Batch config merge: RevyOS merged 20 commits at once, covering SoCs from six vendors: Zhihe, ESWIN, UltraRISC, SpacemiT, XuanTie, and Sophgo. Author dates span roughly a year, indicating this is the wrap-up of a long-running configuration branch, not new development.
  • From running to tuning: The robotics stack’s 11 commits focus on SLAM variance, on-demand encoding, serial port role mapping, and collision bodies. Changes like these don’t show up in release notes, but they determine whether a real robot can run stably.
  • Trend over the past day: Industry is signing land deals, software is packaging, and hardware is making the leaderboard. All three lines are advancing on their own, and their intersection is openRuyi and SpacemiT’s V100 node.
