Date: 2026-11-23　Source: SenseTime.

  • Summary piece: SenseTime’s 11-23 announcement claims more than 10 domestic chips completed Day0 adaptation.
  • Model: SenseNova Loop-9B.
  • Vendors: Biren, Guangbenwei, Cambricon, Hygon, Kunlunxin, Moore Threads.
  • Vendors (cont.): MetaX, Ascend, Xiwang, Xizhi.
  • Model: Roughly 9B parameters.

  • Memory: With 4-bit weights and 8-bit KV cache, a 256K context requires about 15GiB.

  • Each vendor’s path: Cambricon uses vllm_mlu; MetaX uses MXMACA.
  • Xizhi’s path: Uses a proprietary compiler for photonic-electronic partitioning.
  • Relation to FlagOS: The public announcement does not mention FlagOS; noted here only as an ecosystem comparison.
  • Observation: None of the ten vendors attributes its adaptation to a unified stack; Day0 still relies on each vendor’s own engineering team.
