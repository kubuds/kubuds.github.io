Top story: FlagOS Open Computing Global Challenge S2 concludes with 2,025 participants and 354 valid submissions (11-26)

Date: 2026-11-26　Source: PR Newswire Chinese release.

  • Event: Season 2 of the FlagOS Open Computing Global Challenge concludes.
  • Organizers: The Zhongzhi FlagOS community and Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI).
  • Schedule: July to November 2026, four months in total.
  • Scale: 2,025 participants worldwide, including 146 from overseas.
  • Submissions: 72,125 received, 354 valid.
  • Track 1: SGLang framework operators, with 200+ real inference operators released in batches.
  • Track 2: MiniCPM5-2B pushing throughput limits on two chips from Tianshu and MetaX.
  • Team representative: Yusuan Intelligence’s c2flow was first to solve 114 of 211 problems.
  • Results: After judge verification, 100 problems were conquered, with all merged into FlagGems-sglang.
  • Method: Dedicated implementations written for each of five chips for the same operator.
  • Numbers: Average speedup for layernorm_scale_shift_gate_select01 rose from 4.01x to 11.65x.
  • Comparison: In problem 144, packed transfers yielded no gain, while read/write cache hints brought ~24% speedup.
  • Individual: Yang Zhongwu of Nankai University competed solo using an Agent pipeline.
  • Student: Du Xuanzheng of Beihang University used a to-be-verified checklist to fill in missing card conditions.
  • Veteran: Zhou Chunguang competed for the second consecutive season, building methods with a knowledge base.
  • Code corroboration: FlagGems-sglang merged three AMD gfx1100 operators the same day.
  • Under-review corroboration: vllm-plugin-FL has 7 Track 2 contestant PRs under review.
  • Observation: Contest conclusions align with code flow; operator-side work is deposited into FlagGems-sglang.
  • Observation cont’d: Track 2 output is flowing back into the vLLM plugin line.
