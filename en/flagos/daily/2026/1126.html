Top story: Xing4.1-Preview Day0 adaptation across eight chips; per-token latency on NVIDIA platforms drops by up to 20.9% (11-25)

Date: 2026-11-25　Source: FlagOS column.

  • Model: China Telecom AI Technology Co., Ltd. open-sources the Xingchen large model Xing4.1-Preview.
  • Specs: 285B total parameters, roughly 17B activated per token, native 1M context support.
  • Structure: 45-layer Transformer backbone, 42 of which are MoE.
  • Routing: each token selects 8 of 256 experts, plus 1 shared expert.
  • Attention: MLA compresses the KV representation, paired with DSA sparse attention.
  • Adaptation scope: 8 vendors — T-Head, NVIDIA, Moore Threads, Ascend, MetaX, Hygon, Iluvatar, and Kunlunxin.
  • Deliverables: W8A8 and BF16 versions, now available on ModelScope and Hugging Face.
  • Benchmark setup: NVIDIA single node with 8 GPUs, 64 concurrent requests, baseline is the native solution at the same precision.
  • 4K input: output throughput rises from 1862.10 to 1988.23 tokens/s, up 6.8%.
  • 4K latency: average per-token time drops from 30.23 to 27.63 ms, down 8.6%.
  • 16K input: throughput up 4.1%, per-token time down 18.7%.
  • 32K input: throughput up 1.7%, per-token time down 20.9%.
  • Trade-off: average time-to-first-token increases by 11.8%, 21.5%, and 6.0% across the three input tiers.
  • Accuracy: MATH-500, GPQA Diamond, and MMLU align with NVIDIA native.
  • Domestic platforms: in some scenarios, throughput per unit of compute reaches over 70% of NVIDIA native, with a maximum of 181.5%.
  • Operators: 54 FlagGems operators enabled in the 4K scenario.
  • Components: the deployment path uses FlagGems, FlagTree, and vLLM-plugin-FL.
  • Framework: NVIDIA, T-Head, MetaX, Hygon, Iluvatar, and Kunlunxin use vLLM 0.24.0.
  • Framework cont.: Ascend and Moore Threads use SGLang 0.5.18.
  • Observation: this is FlagOS’s first Day0 adaptation this week with complete performance data.
  • Observation cont.: the comparison metric for domestic platforms is throughput per unit of compute, not absolute throughput.
