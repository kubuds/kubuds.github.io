Today’s Highlights: MetaX Hy4 flagship Day0, with FlagOS W8A8 weights released (11-20)

Date: 2026-11-20　Source: 10jqka reposting MetaX official WeChat.　See also: Pandaily.

  • Event: On November 20, Tencent Hunyuan open-sourced its flagship Hy4.
  • Adaptation: MetaX Xiyun C series completed Day0 adaptation the same day and ran it successfully on the platform.
  • Relation to FlagOS: MetaX, together with the Zhongzhi FlagOS community, provided a W8A8 version targeting MetaX compute.
  • Where it lands: Quantized weights are published on ModelScope and Hugging Face, directly usable by developers.
  • MetaX’s claim: The MXMACA stack covers 40-plus frameworks, over 1,000 models, and over 6,000 open-source projects.
  • Operator coverage: The company says it fully supports all 2,410 GPU operators of PyTorch 2.8.
  • Cumulative count: MetaX says it has completed Day0 adaptation for 52 models since December 2025.
  • Discrepancy: The November 18 official site post on Hy4 flash said 51, a difference of one between the two.
  • Test scale: Pandaily reports 4 servers, each with 8 64GB C550 cards, 32 cards in total.
  • Workload: 64 concurrent requests, roughly 4K input and 1K output tokens.
  • Throughput: With decode-graph enabled, aggregate output is 80.98 tokens/s; without it, 52.31, an improvement of about 55%.
  • Latency: First-token latency including queuing is 227.2 seconds and 220.5 seconds; enabling it is actually slower.
  • Context boundary: The deployment configuration’s maximum context is 65,536 tokens, far below the 1 million claimed for Hy4.
  • Unfinished items: Two multimodal evaluations on the model card are marked “in progress.”
  • License: FlagRelease labels it Apache-2.0, while the Tencent Hy License 1.0 treats quantized weights as derivative works.
  • License continued: Large-scale MaaS requires a separate application under the original license; the distribution package’s label alone is not sufficient.
  • Observation: This is FlagOS’s first commercial inference case with third-party media verification.
  • Observation continued: Throughput and latency move in different directions, and 32 cards do not equal production validation; both numbers should be cited together.

  • Next steps: The two sides will advance new model adaptation and explore inclusion in the FlagOS distribution.
  • Next steps continued: They will also jointly release with cloud service partners to push results onto the commercial path.

  • Observation continued: Intellifusion is not yet on the member organization list, but the backend matrix has gained a row.

  • FlagCX: Still at 0.14.1+cuda13.3, the only wheel linking libcudart.so.13.
  • Validation: Both images were built and pushed on h20, and torch inside the container reports CUDA 13.0 available.
  • Validation continued: flagcx registers as privateuseone, and import flag_gems passes.
  • Not done: This line still lacks an app layer, status matrix cells, and extras:.
  • Roadmap: The target release date for FlagOS 2.3 in community #176 remains December 30.
  • Roadmap continued: The first item for SIG Compiler is compiling Qwen3.8 and GLM5.3 Flash into MegaKernel.
  • Observation: The base layer has shifted from switching lines to accounting, indicating that features are freezing.
