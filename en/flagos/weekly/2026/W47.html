FlagOS Weekly Report (2026-11-16 ~ 2026-11-20)

2026-11-20 Kubuds

Reporting period: Monday, November 16, 2026 to Friday, November 20, 2026. 5 working days, 5 daily reports. The 11-16 issue covers a 72-hour window spanning the weekend, from 11-13 06:00 to 11-16 06:00. The other four issues each cover 24 hours, running back-to-back. The behavioral fact window runs from 11-13 06:00 to 11-20 06:00. On the publication date (November 21), additional checks were made on org pushes, merged-PR searches, and four release tags.

Part 1 · Brief

This section is the week’s highlight selection, by default within 800 words (740 words this issue); full entries, itemized lists, and sources are archived in Part 2.

This week, 19 to 20 of the 65 repos had pushes. Across the five issues, 787 commits and 875 merges. The open-review backlog fluctuated between 261 and 318, ending the period at 261.

  • FlagTree 0.7.1 stable line fills in four backends: one tag each on 11-17 and 11-18. The stable release covers ascend3.5, xpu3.6, ppu3.6, enflame3.6. On the pre-release side, a1 and a2 still coexist.
  • The 2.3.0 base swaps out six vendor SDK lines in one week: MetaX moves to maca3.10.1.11, two Enflame tops lines ship for the first time, Cambricon 4.7.6 lands across three layers, and Moore Threads musa4.3.6 is retired. build-infra merged 125 PRs, with no new features.
  • vLLM 0.32.0 application layer opens five backends on the same day: four image tags recorded. Ascend is missing three V2 sampling symbols; T-Head is stuck on a plugin hard import.
  • The host path has become the new battleground: Torch-FL cut Qwen3-0.6B warm-start submission from 211 ms to 28 ms. Latency ratios are 1.17 and 1.18 versus vendor PyTorch.

  • The first complete commercial line appears: on 11-17, BAAI and Qingmao Intelligence launched a commercial Token Factory built on FlagOS. On 11-19, new BIS rules took effect; Enflame hit the limit-down, and five member units fell sharply the same day.

This week’s assessment: The bottleneck for 2.3 has shifted from writing code to platform checklists and acceptance. With only ten days left to rc0, vLLM 0.32 binds six vendors into a synchronized action. If any one vendor’s symbol contract is not aligned, the whole cell stays empty.

  • FlagTree 0.7.1 stable line fills in four backends within a week (11-17~11-18): On 11-17 at 18:23, 0.7.1+ppu3.6 was tagged. On 11-18 at 01:16, 0.7.1+enflame3.6 was tagged.   • vLLM 0.32.0 application layer opens five backends on the same day (11-17): #1851 adds a dependency named
    deps_app.vllm0.32.0 to five lines. The five lines are nvidia-cuda13.3, amd-rocm7.2.4, ascend-cann9.2.1, iluvatar-corex5.0.1, and thead-ppu2.1.0.   • Torch-FL compiles away the “Python tax” (11-14~11-19): #824 compiles FlagGems’ Python-side host logic into a guarded native host program. For Qwen3-0.6B, prefill/decode warm-start CPU submission dropped from 211.51/203.92 ms to 28.46/27.15 ms. Vendor PyTorch is 24.43/22.96 ms, giving latency ratios of 1.17 and 1.18. On 11-19, native hosts for MetaX, Hygon, and Enflame merged on the same day. MetaX: all 86,907,392 logits match, throughput at 83.68% of vendor. Hygon: all 190,831,616 logit elements are bit-identical, with short sequences still at 1.264x and 1.260x. Enflame: two merge rounds at 81.38% and 78.60%, with decode failing to clear the 80% threshold.

  • BAAI and Qingmao Intelligence launch commercial Token Factory (11-17): The launch took place at the 2026 Qingmao AI Partner Conference and Token Factory Annual Meeting. Ecosystem figures: 21 chip companies, 86 ecosystem partners, 35 AI chips. The nine major operator libraries have accumulated over 1,454 Triton operators. Nearly 800 of them reach over 80% of native operator performance on at least 5 domestic chips. On 910C, GLM-5.3 prefill wave throughput improved 3.41%, and TTFT p50 dropped 4.94% at 256K input.

  • Ascend A5 moves from scaffolding to measured results (11-16~11-19): On 11-16, FlagGems-vllm #1245 added BNSD FlashAttention forward for 950DT. On 11-19, FlagGems #9543 added architecture detection and an empty a5/ package. The same day, FlagGems-vllm #1338 merged topk_softplus_sqrt under ascend/a5/ops/. With 256 experts, bf16, and topk=6, 4096 tokens went from about 250 µs to 55 µs. A5 corresponds to Ascend950DT and Ascend950PR, with NpuArch DAV_3510.

  • Five member companies’ shares plunge on the day the new BIS rule takes effect (11-19): The interim final rule “Addressing Advanced Semiconductor Remote Access,” published 11-16, took effect 11-19. Enflame Technology hit the 20% limit down, closing at 364.33 yuan. Turnover that day was 1.962 billion yuan, with a turnover rate of 28.35%. MetaX, Moore Threads, Hygon, Centec, and Hong Kong-listed Iluvatar CoreX fell the same day. FlagOS component code saw no corresponding changes that day.

VI. Trend Observations

  • The foundation layer has shifted from “version patching” to “line replacement and retirement”: This week six SDK lines were replaced, one retired, and one newly added. None of the 125 merges in build-infra added a new feature. The working pattern is “one line per card,” with a uniform rhythm: base lands first, runtime follows, the index is completed, and then wheels are added. With ten days to rc0, this pace means Cambricon 4.7.6 most likely will not make the 2.3.0 default matrix.

  • The performance narrative has shifted to the host path and context reuse: Torch-FL pushes warm-start CPU submission to 1.17x and 1.18x that of the vendor PyTorch, entering the 80% threshold in #822. Ascend MegaMHC’s Eager without pre-bound context reaches only 0.648x, showing that the gain comes from startup and workspace reuse rather than pure kernel arithmetic. Cross-chip comparisons must now include a host baseline, or the numbers are not comparable.

  • The acceptance side has become the bottleneck: KernelGen generated 43 operators in one day, while 40 submissions on the same day were fixing precision and timeouts across vendor backends. The review queue stands between 261 and 318 PRs, with Kunlunxin’s “287 ops fix” alone touching 203 files and +53022 lines. Operators are being added faster than they are aligned, and review throughput determines the coverage of 2.3.

  • Upstream versions force downstream into synchronized action: Five vendors opened their gates on the same day for vLLM 0.32, with six under review, and five vendors closed out SGLang 0.5.18 in the same week. Neither blocker is FlagOS’s own code; both are symbol contracts. Examples include missing exports such as v2_apply_penalties, hard imports such as flash_attn_3._C, and ECCL’s 64-bit dtype boundary. Such issues can only be explicitly accounted for in the matrix, not masked by retries.

  • New hardware is organized into directories by generation, and compiler interfaces are changing generations: Ascend has moved from “one backend” to generation-based directories such as _ascend/a5, where A5 corresponds to 950DT and 950PR. At the same time, the TLE pipeline iterator was removed, and three libraries—FlagGems, FlagGems-vllm, and FlagAttention—switched to tl.range on the same day. Upper-layer operators must follow compiler interfaces, and this has become the norm.

  • The news side and the code side continue to diverge, but this week’s industry narrative has substantive content: Component-name queries returned zero hits for five consecutive windows, and public coverage is still supplied by member organizations’ capital and product news. Token Factory is the only industry event this week with complete technical figures. The new BIS rules are the only event that changed the external environment. The daily report’s criteria remain first-hand data from commits, PRs, and the packaging matrix.
