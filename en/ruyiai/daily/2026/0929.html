Today’s Highlight: Three Caffe RVV operator PRs opened, 15 MXNet-series PRs under review — the classic-framework adaptation track enters hands-on phase

Date: 2026-09-23 ~ 2026-09-28　Source: caffe PR #1, caffe PR #2, caffe PR #3, MXNet PR list

Between 14:56 and 14:59 Beijing time on 09-28 (within three minutes), contributor Xlawy opened three consecutive RVV operator PRs in the caffe repo (one of two classic-framework repos onboarded on 09-23) — the first substantive code activity in either repo since onboarding:

  • #1 im2col (+113 / -9, 2 files): Splits im2col’s output-row gathering into a scalar helper and an RVV float overload; each row is split into zero-padding and in-bounds segments, selecting unit-stride or strided vector loads based on stride_w, using variable-length e32,m4 blocking; also adapts Protobuf’s single-argument SetTotalBytesLimit API to version 3.6.0 and above.
  • #2 float CPU max pooling (+167 / -24, 2 files): Processes max-pooling inner columns with e32,m4 blocking, using strided loads and strict-greater-than comparisons to update the maximum value and winning-position offset, preserving first-occurrence tie semantics and the -1 sentinel, and unrolling the three-column window; boundaries still go through scalar code.
  • #3 float vPowx (+166 / -1, 2 files): Adds an RVV specialization for non-MKL vPowx<float> — enabling vector log/exp approximation (with double-precision intermediates) when all inputs are normal numbers and the exponent is in [-1, 1]; exponents of 0 and 1 go through direct vector operations; supports blocked in-place calls; falls back to scalar for the entire call when the domain check fails.

All three add specializations guarded by RVV macros and retain scalar fallbacks for non-RVV builds; all are currently open with no review records. Earlier activity on the same track concentrated in the MXNet repo: over two days from 09-23 to 09-24, three contributors opened a cumulative 15 PRs (ww8191201-coder 11, Xlawy 2, 6eanut 2), of which 11 are RVV operator vectorizations — GELU(erf) backward, ReLU backward, softmax, log-softmax, softmin, CTC log-softmax, im2col, col2im, batched cumsum, nearest-neighbor upsampling, and float32 minimum reduction; the other 4 cover performance and build fundamentals (strength reduction of index arithmetic in broadcast_reduce and reduce_with_axis, removal of volatile from the sum reducer, and a request for -fno-math-errno in non-MSVC builds). Several PR descriptions directly cite the SG2044 (RVV 1.0, GCC 15.1) testing context. This series was not covered in previous windows and is recorded here together with the new Caffe activity.

As of the end of the window, all 18 PRs across the two repos remain open, with none merged. Combined with the 09-24 issue’s note to watch whether the two repos were “archived, an adaptation baseline, or a new starting point for work,” this batch provides a provisional answer: the two repos are being used as hands-on bases for RVV adaptation of classic frameworks, with the MXNet series covering the inference backbone operator surface and the Caffe series focusing on convolution and activation infrastructure. The same night, compiler-side RVV activity was carried by tilelang-riscv PR #4 (see 1.1), making RVV the most concentrated technical theme within the organization that day.

V. Trend Observations

5.1 Classic Frameworks: RVV Takes Shape on Two Fronts — From “Merged” to “PR Pile-Up”

When the two repos were merged on 09-23, their purpose was noted as pending observation; within a week, MXNet filed 15 PRs (three contributors) covering 11 RVV operators plus 4 performance and build foundations, and Caffe followed on 09-28 with three RVV operator PRs — the “adaptation” track has now formally made its move in the classic-framework dimension. On 09-25 (the 15th day of the eighth lunar month, Mid-Autumn Festival) and the following weekend, submissions from the three organizations came to a near standstill; on 09-28, the first working day after the holiday, output concentrated: 5 commits to the openRuyi main repo, 3 to kiwi-image, 2 to the VS Code extension plus the 0.1.7 release, and ruyiai-daily and the forum news post both resumed the same day — the holiday-driven “trough and rebound” is fully visible across two windows.

Three tooling lines advanced at their own cadence during the window: the VS Code extension 0.1.7 went stable (8 days after beta), Ruyi Imager updated its nightly channel, and triton-riscv produced its weekly nightly build on schedule; correspondingly, the ruyi-tuner 1.9 line has remained in a “no release but changes in flight” state for two consecutive windows.
