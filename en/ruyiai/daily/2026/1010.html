Today’s Highlight: Dual-track toolchain sync — Triton-RISCV patch adapts to new LLVM APIs (#86 merged), LLVM riscv branch completes another nightly replay

Date: 2026-10-09 ~ 2026-10-10　Sources: triton-riscv PR #86, merge commit e00fdd60, llvm-project riscv commit list, head commit 8f38c047, PR #25, PR #28

triton-riscv completed its first PR merge since 09-14 (#81): contributor trdthg opened and merged PR #86 “[patches] Support newer LLVM symbol and inline-asm APIs” around noon on 10-09 (1 file, +111 lines; roughly 20 minutes from open to merge). The context was renovate-maintained Buddy MLIR dependency upgrade PR #84 — bumping buddy-compiler/buddy-mlir from nightly/v0.0.9 to nightly/v0.0.11. Since v0.0.11 carries a newer LLVM version, its MLIR APIs (such as symbol and inline-asm interfaces) are no longer compatible with the pinned-frontend Triton, causing CI across x64 and riscv64 and all Python versions to fail at the build stage. #86 patches in support for the new LLVM symbol and inline-asm APIs, unblocking the issue upon merge (#84 was subsequently refreshed that afternoon and remains open pending inclusion).

On the LLVM side, the llvm-project riscv branch completed another full replay of the patch stack late on 10-09: 26 local patches were re-landed with new committer timestamps (pushed around 23:00 Beijing time), with the head remaining #31 “A100 HP/SP and pack/unpack lowering and scheduling calibration” (8f38c047); the stack composition matches the previous round (including BOSC AME, Buckyball, IME lowering to A100 dot, xtame and other vendor extension patches, plus SyncBots integration records). After the replay, the update timestamps of two FPGA backend PRs (#25 “BOSCAME FPGA backend”, #28 “xboscame FPGA”) were refreshed at 00:53 on 10-10, awaiting review progress under the new baseline.

Summary: In this window, two toolchain maintenance tracks landed in sequence — the LLVM side completed its nightly replay, and the Triton side completed patch adaptation to the new LLVM APIs; together they constitute the stability engineering of the RISC-V AI compiler stack — “replay—adapt—build pipeline” — beneath a rapidly evolving LLVM upstream. The merge was picked up the same day in the ruyiai-daily 10-10 issue, which noted that it “directly concerns RuyiAI’s Triton→Buddy/MLIR build chain, resolving interface incompatibilities after the LLVM upgrade.”
